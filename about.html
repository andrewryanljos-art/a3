A Commitment to Kickwheels

Diningviora was established with a singular, unwavering mission: to rescue the fading art of manual kickwheel throwing and introduce it to luxury ceramic design. In a world increasingly saturated by quick synthetic plastics and mass-molded factory porcelain, we dedicate our studio to long-staple organic kaolin clay and raw mineral oxides. We believe a dinner plate is not simply a dish; it is a structural representation of physical ease, tactile beauty, and potter craft.

Each clay block is sourced individually from sustainable mineral beds in Normandy. Once received at our New York showroom, the pottery is wedged, thrown, foot-trimmed, and wood-fired entirely by hand. Organic glaze uses wood ash and mineral oxides instead of harsh chemical lead, protecting your skin and food surfaces. It is this minute attention to microscopic details that allows our master potters to sustain a Lifetime Restoration Guarantee.

Our Three Ceramic Pillars

01. Organic Kaolin

We refine our kaolin minerals three times in water to isolate titanium impurities.

02. Foot Trimming

Potters cut each plate ring foot manually to ensure weight balance on desks.

03. Reduction Firings

We choke kiln oxygen at peak heats, forcing bronze molecules to rise on surfaces.

The Pottery Future

As Diningviora looks to the upcoming decade, we remain anchored in our slow-craft principles. We maintain a small studio of certified weavers, limiting our global output to a few hundred dinner sets per year. This exclusivity ensures that every client registered receives the undivided focus and care of our master potters. Diningviora is a lifetime commitment to kickwheels, hand-trimmed panels, and heritage preservation.
